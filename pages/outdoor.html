Syzygium (Brush Cherry)

Syzygium (Brush Cherry)

Scientific Name: Syzygium myrtifolium

Price (Approx.): ₹250 – ₹600 (depending on size)

Description:
Syzygium (Brush Cherry) is an attractive evergreen shrub or small tree known for its vibrant red young leaves that gradually turn glossy green as they mature. It is widely used for hedges, landscaping, gardens, and decorative borders because of its dense foliage and easy maintenance.

Care Tips:
☀️Light: Full sun to partial shade
💧Water: Water regularly; keep the soil slightly moist but avoid waterlogging.
🌱Soil: Well-draining, fertile soil
🌡️Temperature: 20–35°C
🌿 Fertilizer: Apply a balanced fertilizer every 2–3 months for healthy growth.

Benefits :

✅ Enhances landscape beauty.
✅Acts as a natural privacy screen.
✅Helps improve air quality.
✅Low-maintenance and suitable for home gardens and parks.

Propagation :
Stem Cuttings (Best Method): Take a healthy 10–15 cm semi-hardwood cutting, remove the lower leaves, dip in rooting hormone (optional), and plant in moist, well-draining soil. Roots usually develop in 4–8 weeks.
Seeds: Sow fresh seeds in moist potting mix. Keep warm and moist until they germinate, though this method is slower than cuttings.

Interesting Fact :New leaves emerge in a striking bright red color before turning green.
Excellent for creating privacy hedges and decorative garden borders.
Can also be grown as a bonsai due to its dense foliage and easy pruning.

Golden Cypress

Golden Cypress

Scientific Name: Cupressus macrocarpa 'Goldcrest'

Price (Approx.): ₹300 – ₹800 (depending on size)

Description:
Golden Cypress is a beautiful evergreen ornamental plant with bright golden-green foliage. It is commonly used for home gardens, landscaping, balconies, and decorative pots. Its fresh lemon-like fragrance and attractive appearance make it a popular choice for outdoor decoration.

Care Tips:
☀️ Sunlight: Full sunlight to partial shade
💧 Water: Water regularly, but avoid overwatering
🌱 Soil: Well-drained, fertile soil
✂️ Maintenance: Trim occasionally to maintain shape

Benefits :

✅ Enhances the beauty of gardens and entrances
✅Evergreen throughout the year
✅Low-maintenance ornamental plant
✅Suitable for pots and landscaping

Propagation :
Semi-hardwood cuttings – The most common and effective propagation method.
Seeds – Possible, but growth is slow and plants may not retain the exact characteristics of the parent plant.
Grafting – Commonly used in commercial nurseries.

Interesting Fact :Golden Cypress is an evergreen conifer that naturally releases a fresh lemon-like fragrance when its foliage is gently rubbed or crushed.


Lantana

Lantana

Scientific Name: Lantana camara

Price (Approx.): ₹150 – ₹400 (depending on size)

Description:
Lantana is a colorful flowering plant that produces clusters of small flowers in shades of pink, yellow, orange, red, and white. It blooms throughout the year in warm climates and is widely used in gardens, parks, and landscaping due to its vibrant appearance.

Care Tips:
☀️ Sunlight: Full sunlight (6–8 hours daily)
💧 Water: Water moderately; allow the soil to dry slightly between watering
🌱 Soil: Well-drained garden soil
✂️ Maintenance: Remove dried flowers and prune regularly for better growth

Benefits :

✅ Produces attractive, colorful flowers
✅ Attracts butterflies and bees
✅ Drought-tolerant and easy to maintain
✅ Ideal for gardens, borders, and decorative landscapes

Propagation : Stem Cuttings – The easiest and most common method of propagation.
Seeds – Can also be grown from seeds, though plants may vary from the parent.
Best Time – Spring and the rainy season are ideal for successful propagation.

Interesting Fact :
Lantana flowers change color as they mature, making a single flower cluster display multiple colors at the same time. They are also highly attractive to butterflies and other pollinators.


Mango Tree

Mango Tree

Scientific Name: Mangifera indica

Price (Approx.): ₹250 – ₹1,000 (depending on variety and plant size)

Description:
Mango Tree is a popular tropical fruit tree known as the "King of Fruits." It produces sweet, juicy mangoes and provides excellent shade with its dense green canopy. It is widely grown in gardens, farms, and home landscapes.

Care Tips:
☀️ Sunlight: Full sunlight (6–8 hours daily)
💧 Water:Water regularly during the growing season; reduce watering in winter
🌱 Soil: Well-drained, fertile soil
🌡️ Temperature: 24°C–35°C

Benefits :

✅ Produces delicious and nutritious fruits
✅ Provides excellent shade
✅ Long-living and low maintenance once established
✅ Attracts birds and pollinators

Propagation : Mango trees are commonly propagated through grafting to maintain fruit quality. They can also be grown from seeds, but seed-grown trees take longer to produce fruits.

Interesting Fact :
The Mango Tree is the National Tree of Bangladesh, and mangoes are often called the "King of Fruits" because of their rich taste and popularity.
