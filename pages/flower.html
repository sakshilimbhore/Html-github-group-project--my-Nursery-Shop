White Plumeria (Frangipani / Champa)

White Plumeria (Frangipani / Champa)

Scientific Name: Plumeria alba

Price (Approx.): ₹200 – ₹700 (depending on size)

Description:
White Plumeria (Frangipani) is a beautiful tropical flowering plant known for its fragrant white flowers with bright yellow centers. It blooms throughout the warm months and is widely grown in gardens, parks, temples, and landscapes for its ornamental beauty and pleasant fragrance.

Care Tips:
☀️ Light: Full sunlight (6–8 hours daily).
💧 Water: Water moderately; allow the soil to dry slightly between watering.
🌱 Soil: Well-draining sandy or loamy soil.
🌡️ Temperature: 20–35°C.
🌿 Fertilizer: Apply a balanced flowering fertilizer every 2–3 months during the growing season.

Benefits :

✅ Produces beautiful, fragrant flowers throughout the season.
✅ Enhances the beauty of gardens, balconies, and landscapes.
✅ Attracts butterflies and pollinators.
✅ Low-maintenance and drought-tolerant once established.

Propagation :
Stem Cuttings (Best Method): Take a healthy 25–30 cm mature stem cutting, allow it to dry for a few days, and plant it in well-draining soil. Roots usually develop within 4–8 weeks.
Seeds: Sow fresh seeds in moist potting mix. Seed-grown plants take longer to flower than those grown from cuttings.

Interesting Fact :
Plumeria flowers are highly fragrant and commonly used in garlands and perfumes.
The flowers symbolize beauty, peace, and new beginnings in many cultures.
A healthy Plumeria plant can bloom for many years with proper care.

Brahma Kamal (Queen of the Night)

Brahma Kamal (Queen of the Night)

Scientific Name: Epiphyllum oxypetalum

Price (Approx.): ₹250 – ₹700

Description:
Brahma Kamal, also known as the Queen of the Night, is a beautiful flowering cactus famous for its large, fragrant white flowers that bloom only at night. It is a popular ornamental plant and is admired for its rare and spectacular blooms.

Care Tips:
☀️ Light: Bright indirect sunlight or partial shade
💧 Water: Water moderately and allow the soil to dry between watering
🌡️ Temperature: 18°C–30°C
🪴 Soil: Well-drained cactus or succulent potting mix

Benefits :

✅ Produces stunning fragrant flowers that bloom at night.
✅ Adds beauty and elegance to homes and gardens.
✅ Easy to grow with proper care.
✅ A popular ornamental plant with long-lasting green stems.
✅ Considered a symbol of purity, peace, and good fortune in many cultures.

Propagation : Brahma Kamal is commonly propagated through stem cuttings. Allow the cutting to dry for 2–3 days before planting it in well-drained soil. Roots usually develop within a few weeks.

Interesting Fact : Brahma Kamal flowers bloom only once at night and usually wilt by the next morning. Their sweet fragrance and short blooming period make them one of the most fascinating flowering plants.


Marigold (African Marigold)

Marigold (African Marigold)

Scientific Name: Tagetes erecta

Price (Approx.): ₹50 – ₹250

Description:
Marigold is a bright and cheerful flowering plant known for its vibrant orange and yellow blooms. It is widely grown in gardens, parks, and homes because of its long flowering season, pleasant appearance, and easy maintenance. Marigold flowers are also commonly used for festivals, decorations, and religious ceremonies.

Care Tips:
☀️ Light: Full sunlight (6–8 hours daily)
💧 Water: Water regularly but avoid overwatering
🌡️ Temperature: 18°C–30°C
🪴 Soil: Well-drained, fertile soil rich in organic matter

Benefits :

✅ Produces beautiful and colorful flowers throughout the season.
✅ Attracts butterflies and other beneficial pollinators.
✅ Helps repel certain garden pests naturally.
✅ Ideal for flower beds, borders, balconies, and pots.
✅ Commonly used in garlands, festivals, weddings, and decorations.

Propagation : Marigold is mainly propagated by seeds. Sow the seeds in well-drained soil, keep the soil moist, and seedlings usually appear within 5–10 days.

Interesting Fact : Marigold is one of the most popular flowering plants in India. Its bright flowers bloom for several months and are widely used during festivals like Diwali, Ganesh Chaturthi, and Navratri.


Ixora (Jungle Geranium)

Ixora (Jungle Geranium)

Scientific Name: Ixora coccinea

Price (Approx.): ₹150 – ₹500

Description:
Ixora is a beautiful evergreen flowering shrub known for its bright clusters of red, pink, orange, yellow, or white flowers. It blooms throughout the year in warm climates and is widely used for gardens, hedges, and landscape decoration.

Care Tips:
☀️ Light: Full sunlight to partial shade
💧 Water: Water regularly and keep the soil slightly moist
🌡️ Temperature: 20°C–35°C
🪴 Soil: Well-drained, slightly acidic, nutrient-rich soil

Benefits :

✅ Produces colorful flowers throughout the year.
✅ Attracts butterflies and bees to the garden.
✅ Ideal for hedges, borders, and ornamental landscaping.
✅ Easy to maintain with regular watering and pruning.
✅ Adds vibrant color and beauty to homes, parks, and gardens.

Propagation : Ixora is commonly propagated through stem cuttings or seeds. Stem cuttings root easily when planted in moist, well-drained soil.

Interesting Fact : Ixora flowers bloom in dense clusters and can flower almost all year in tropical climates. The plant is also used in traditional Ayurvedic medicine for its medicinal properties.
