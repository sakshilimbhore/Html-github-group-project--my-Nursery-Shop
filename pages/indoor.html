Money Plant (Golden Pothos)

Money Plant (Golden Pothos)

Scientific Name: Epipremnum aureum

Price (Approx.): ₹199 – ₹399

Description:
The Money Plant is one of the most popular indoor plants because of its attractive heart-shaped green leaves. It is easy to grow, requires little maintenance, and helps improve indoor air quality.

Care Tips:
☀️ Light: Bright indirect sunlight
💧 Water: Water only when the top soil feels dry
🌡️ Temperature: 18°C–30°C
🪴 Soil: Well-drained potting mix

Benefits :

✅ Helps purify indoor air.
✅ Easy to grow and maintain.
✅ Adds a fresh and elegant look to homes and offices.
✅ Can be grown in both soil and water.
✅ Believed to bring good luck, prosperity, and positive energy according to Feng Shui.

Propagation :Money Plant can be easily propagated from stem cuttings. Place a healthy cutting with a node in water or moist soil, and roots usually develop within 2–3 weeks.

Interesting Fact :Money Plant is one of the easiest houseplants to grow and is considered a symbol of wealth, prosperity, and good fortune in many cultures.

Areca Palm

Areca Palm

Scientific Name: Dypsis lutescens

Price (Approx.):₹400 – ₹1,200

Description:
Areca Palm is a graceful indoor plant with long, arching, feathery green leaves. It is one of the best air-purifying plants and is widely used to decorate homes, offices, hotels, and commercial spaces. Its lush tropical appearance makes any indoor space look fresh and elegant.

Care Tips:
☀️ Sunlight: Bright indirect sunlight
💧 Water: Water 2–3 times a week or when the top soil becomes dry
🌱 Soil: Well-drained, fertile potting mix
🌡️ Temperature: 18°C–30°C
💦 Humidity: Prefers moderate to high humidity
🌿 Fertilizer: Feed once a month during spring and summer

Benefits :

✅ Removes indoor air pollutants
✅ Increases humidity naturally
✅ Enhances home and office décor
✅ Easy to maintain
✅ Creates a fresh and tropical atmosphere

Propagation :Areca Palm is propagated by dividing healthy offshoots from the parent plant during repotting.

Interesting Fact :Areca Palm is recognized by NASA as one of the best indoor plants for improving air quality. It helps remove pollutants such as xylene and toluene while adding moisture to the air.


Lucky Bamboo

Lucky Bamboo

Scientific Name: Dracaena sanderiana

Price (Approx.):₹250 – ₹800

Description:
Lucky Bamboo is a popular indoor ornamental plant that symbolizes good luck, prosperity, happiness, and positive energy. Despite its name, it is not a true bamboo. It is widely used in homes, offices, hotels, and as a decorative gift. Lucky Bamboo grows well in both water and soil, making it one of the easiest plants to maintain.

Care Tips:
☀️ Sunlight: Bright indirect sunlight
💧 Water: Change the water every 7–10 days if grown in water
🌱 Soil: Can be grown in water with pebbles or in well-drained soil
🌡️ Temperature: 18°C–30°C
💦 Humidity: Prefers moderate humidity
🌿 Fertilizer: Use a small amount of liquid fertilizer every 2–3 months

Benefits :

✅ Brings good luck and prosperity according to Feng Shui
✅ Easy to grow and maintain
✅ Enhances home and office décor
✅ Improves indoor air quality
✅ Perfect for gifting on special occasions

Propagation :Lucky Bamboo can be propagated by cutting healthy stems and placing them in clean water until new roots develop.

Interesting Fact :
Different numbers of Lucky Bamboo stalks have different meanings:
2 Stalks: Love and Relationships ❤️
3 Stalks: Happiness, Wealth & Long Life 🍀
5 Stalks: Good Health 🌿
8 Stalks: Growth and Success 📈
9 Stalks: Good Fortune ✨


Monstera Deliciosa

Monstera Deliciosa

Scientific Name: Monstera deliciosa

Price (Approx.): ₹500 – ₹1,500

Description:
Monstera Deliciosa is a stylish indoor plant known for its large, glossy green leaves with natural holes and splits. It is a favorite choice for homes, offices, and modern interiors because of its tropical appearance and low-maintenance nature.

Care Tips:
☀️ Sunlight: Bright indirect sunlight
💧 Water: Water when the top soil feels dry
🌱 Soil: Well-drained, nutrient-rich potting mix
🌡️ Temperature: 18°C–30°C

Benefits :

✅ Purifies indoor air
✅ Enhances home and office décor
✅ Easy to grow and maintain
✅ Creates a fresh, tropical atmosphere

Propagation :Monstera Deliciosa is commonly propagated through stem cuttings. Cut a healthy stem with at least one node and place it in water or well-drained soil. Once roots develop, transplant it into a pot. Spring and summer are the best seasons for propagation.

Interesting Fact :
Monstera Deliciosa is called the "Swiss Cheese Plant" because its mature leaves naturally develop unique holes and splits. These openings help the plant withstand strong winds and allow sunlight to reach lower leaves in tropical forests.
