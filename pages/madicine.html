🌱 Plant Categories:- Medicinal Plants

Aloe Vera

Aloe Vera

Scientific Name: Aloe barbadensis Miller

Price (Approx.): ₹100 – ₹300

Description:
Aloe Vera is a popular succulent plant known for its thick, fleshy green leaves filled with a soothing gel. It is widely grown as both an indoor and outdoor plant because of its medicinal properties, easy maintenance, and attractive appearance.

Care Tips:
☀️ Light: Bright sunlight or partial shade
💧 Water: Water only when the soil is completely dry
🌡️ Temperature: 18°C–30°C
🪴 Soil: Well-drained sandy or cactus potting mix

Benefits :

✅ Gel helps soothe minor burns and skin irritation.
✅ Improves the beauty of homes and offices.
✅ Easy to grow and requires very little maintenance.
✅ Helps improve indoor air quality.
✅ Widely used in skincare, cosmetics, and herbal products.

Propagation : Aloe Vera is commonly propagated through offsets (pups) that grow around the base of the parent plant. Separate the pups and plant them in well-drained soil.

Interesting Fact : Aloe Vera has been used for thousands of years for its healing properties. It is often called the "Plant of Immortality" because of its many medicinal uses.

Holy Basil (Tulsi)

Holy Basil (Tulsi)

Scientific Name: Ocimum tenuiflorum (Ocimum sanctum)

Price (Approx.): ₹80 – ₹250

Description:
Tulsi, also known as Holy Basil, is one of the most sacred and medicinal plants in India. It is valued for its aromatic leaves, health benefits, and spiritual significance. Tulsi is easy to grow and is commonly planted in homes and gardens.

Care Tips:
☀️ Light: Full sunlight (5–6 hours daily)
💧 Water: Water regularly but avoid waterlogging
🌡️ Temperature: 20°C–35°C
🪴 Soil: Well-drained, fertile soil rich in organic matter

Benefits :

✅ Known for its medicinal and healing properties.
✅ Helps improve air quality around the home.
✅ Leaves are commonly used in herbal tea and Ayurvedic remedies.
✅ Repels mosquitoes and some harmful insects naturally.
✅ Considered a sacred plant and symbolizes purity and positivity.

Propagation : Tulsi is commonly propagated through seeds or stem cuttings. Seeds germinate within 7–14 days when planted in moist, well-drained soil.

Interesting Fact : Tulsi is known as the "Queen of Herbs" and has been used in Ayurveda for thousands of years. It is worshipped in many Indian households and is believed to bring health, peace, and prosperity.


Indian Borage (Ajwain Leaf Plant)

Indian Borage (Ajwain Leaf Plant)

Scientific Name: Plectranthus amboinicus

Price (Approx.): ₹100 – ₹300

Description:
Indian Borage, also known as the Ajwain Leaf Plant or Mexican Mint, is a fragrant medicinal herb with thick, soft, and fleshy green leaves. It is widely grown in home gardens for its culinary uses, pleasant aroma, and traditional medicinal benefits.

Care Tips:
☀️ Light: Bright sunlight to partial shade
💧 Water: Water moderately; allow the topsoil to dry before watering
🌡️ Temperature: 18°C–35°C
🪴 Soil: Well-drained, fertile potting mix

Benefits :

✅ Leaves are widely used in traditional herbal remedies.
✅ Easy to grow and requires very little maintenance.
✅ Pleasant aroma makes it a popular kitchen herb.
✅ Can be grown in pots, balconies, and home gardens.
✅ Rich in antioxidants and commonly used in herbal teas.

Propagation : Indian Borage is easily propagated through stem cuttings. Place a healthy cutting in moist soil, and roots usually develop within 2–3 weeks.

Interesting Fact : Indian Borage leaves have a strong oregano-like fragrance and are commonly used in Indian households for home remedies and cooking. The plant grows quickly and spreads easily.


Neem

Neem

Scientific Name: Azadirachta indica

Price (Approx.): ₹80 – ₹300

Description:
Neem is a fast-growing evergreen tree well known for its medicinal, environmental, and agricultural benefits. It has bright green leaves and is widely planted in gardens, parks, and along roadsides. Neem is valued for its natural healing properties and ability to purify the air.

Care Tips:
☀️ Light: Full sunlight (6–8 hours daily)
💧 Water: Water regularly when young; mature trees require less watering
🌡️ Temperature: 20°C–40°C
🪴 Soil: Well-drained sandy or loamy soil

Benefits :

✅ Purifies the air and provides excellent shade.
✅ Leaves, bark, seeds, and oil have medicinal uses.
✅ Acts as a natural insect and pest repellent.
✅ Helps improve soil quality and supports biodiversity.
✅ Widely used in Ayurveda, skincare, and organic farming.

Propagation : Neem is commonly propagated by fresh seeds. Seeds germinate within 1–3 weeks when planted in moist, well-drained soil. It can also be propagated through stem cuttings, though seed propagation is more common.

Interesting Fact : Neem is often called the "Village Pharmacy" because almost every part of the tree has medicinal value. It has been used in traditional Ayurvedic medicine for thousands of years.
